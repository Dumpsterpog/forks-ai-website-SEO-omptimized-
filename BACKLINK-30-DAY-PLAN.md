## Link-worthy resource ready for sharing

https://forksai.app/resources/student-study-kit — original editable weekly revision, practice-question error log and flashcard checklist templates. Free, ungated, usable with any app, and redistribution allowed without mandatory attribution. Start the campaign only once the deployed page and downloads return 200.

## Work and measurement by week

Week 1 (days 1–7): publish and verify the resource, recover missing indexed URLs, record the baseline referring domains from Ahrefs and Search Console Links, and identify 30 relevant contacts. Separate existing relationships from cold prospects. Research the exact page and the intended student audience before selecting each contact.

Week 2 (days 8–14): send 10 individualized resource suggestions to student societies, tutors or academic-skills editors after the user authorizes sending from an account. Ask whether the free planning kit is useful to their students; do not require a link, request a followed attribute, or offer a reciprocal link. Prepare an accurate product listing for Product Hunt and AlternativeTo where eligible and not already listed.

Week 3 (days 15–21): approach 10 additional relevant editors or creators with a useful original contribution, such as a worked lecture-to-revision example, and disclose affiliation. Ask existing users who publish study-resource pages for feedback. Turn real user feedback into better examples; do not invent testimonials, affiliations or usage statistics.

Week 4 (days 22–30): follow up once on unanswered relevant outreach, approach the remaining 10 contacts, and record published links, relevant referral visits and completed signups. Inspect broken links and indexing again. Compare equal time periods and query cohorts, not aggregate DR alone.

Activity targets: 30 researched contacts, 20–30 personalized messages if authorized, 2 accurate discovery listings if eligible, and one follow-up per contact at most. Working outcome goal: 3–8 genuine relevant referring domains. That range is a campaign goal, not a promise. Record DR weekly, but optimize for real students, editorial relevance and conversions.

## Researched routes and their limits

- Product Hunt: https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch — documented self-submission, login required. Prepare product URL, accurate tagline, description, real demo images and maker comment. Submission and link attributes are not yet verified; a listing is a discovery opportunity, not guaranteed ranking credit.
- AlternativeTo: https://alternativeto.net/faq/ — software discovery/community route. Check existing FORKSAI listing, current contribution rules and moderation before proposing an alternative to study tools. No listing has been created here and no followed backlink is promised.
- Dartmouth Academic Skills resource library: https://students.dartmouth.edu/academic-skills/learning-resources/academic-resource-library — example of a relevant curated educational resource collection. It does not establish that outside commercial submissions are accepted. Treat as an editorial-fit research lead only, not a confirmed guest-post opportunity.
- UNC Learning Center: https://learningcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/studying-101-study-smarter-not-harder/ — relevant study guidance and resource context. Research whether a useful outside worksheet suggestion is appropriate; no acceptance or endorsement is implied.
- Student societies at institutions where the founder already has a real contact: prioritize their resource or revision pages. Ask the founder for specific introductions; do not scrape member email lists.
- Independent tutors and student creators already covering active recall or revision planning: find a specific existing guide where a reusable kit genuinely helps readers. Validate audience and editorial fit before contact.

These are research routes, not secured backlinks or a verified outreach list. No messages, directory submissions or third-party posts have been sent or published by this task.

Personalize every bracketed detail from a real source. Do not send the template unchanged.

## Avoid

Do not buy followed link bundles, automate directory spam, require keyword-rich links in redistributed templates, or trade links primarily for rankings. Genuine sponsorships should be qualified as sponsored/nofollow; they are audience campaigns rather than purchased ranking credit.
